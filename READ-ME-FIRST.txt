WHAT CHANGED
------------
The page was capped at 1080px wide. On a 1920px screen that filled 56%
of the monitor, and on a 2560px screen 42% — which is why it looked like
a phone layout stranded in the middle of a desktop. The cap is now
1280px, and the donate page's own three 1120px caps went with it.

But widening the page on its own would have made the site HARDER to
read, not easier, and that is worth understanding before anyone asks
for it to go wider still.


WHY IT IS NOT FULL WIDTH
------------------------
A line of text longer than about 85 characters is hard to read, because
the eye loses its place travelling back to the start of the next line.
It is the same problem as text being too small, and it affects the same
people.

The lines were ALREADY too long inside the old 1080px:

  prayer times   147 characters
  madrasah       141
  about          122
  nikah           99

At 1280px those would have gone to 167, 156 and 125. So paragraphs are
now capped separately, at about 70 characters, no matter how wide the
page gets. Timetables, card grids and the header have no such limit —
they have no reason to, and they are what now fills the extra space.

If anyone asks for the site to go edge to edge, this is the answer: the
empty space at the sides is not waste, it is what keeps the sentences
readable. The furniture can widen; the sentences cannot.


ONE THING THAT BROKE AND WAS FIXED
----------------------------------
Capping the paragraphs knocked four CENTRED paragraphs off centre — a
narrow block still sits at the left of its parent unless told otherwise.
On the media page that left 723px of blank space to the right of the
text. Fixed, and now guarded.


THE NEW TEST FILE
-----------------
_test/measure_test.py checks four things on all 29 pages, at desktop and
phone size: no line over 82 characters, no centred text off centre, the
page really is wider on a big screen, and it still fits a phone. Each
check was proved by deliberately breaking it and confirming it failed.

It never touches the live site or the database.
