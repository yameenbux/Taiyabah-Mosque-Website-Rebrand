TAIYABAH MASJID — charity collections (chanda) go online
========================================================
14 September 2026

10 files. Upload on top of the three earlier zips.

The paper CHARITY DATA FORM and the poster with two mobile numbers are
replaced by a form on the website that lands in the Admin Centre.


HOW TO UPLOAD
-------------
1. Open the repository on github.com, on the branch you are using.
2. "Add file" -> "Upload files".
3. Unzip this first, then drag the CONTENTS of the "taiyabah-collections"
   folder in — not the folder itself, and not this text file. Keep the
   folder structure (collection/, collections/, db/, _test/, portals/).
4. Commit.

NOTHING HAS TO BE RUN ON THE DATABASE. Migrations 030 and 031 are in the
db/ folder for the record, but they are ALREADY APPLIED to the live
Supabase project. The form works the moment these files are live.


WHERE IT SITS ON THE WEBSITE
----------------------------
THE LINK TO HAND OUT:   taiyabahmasjid.com/collection

That is the one to read out on the phone, put on a poster, or paste into
WhatsApp. It is short enough to say once and spell correctly.

In the site's own navigation it appears in two places:

  * CONTACT — the main one. Contact is where an outside organisation
    approaching the masjid looks. Services is headed "what the masjid
    offers the community", which is a Bolton Muslim asking what they can
    get; a charity collection runs the other way.

  * SERVICES — a tile as well, because somebody collecting for their own
    village's madrasah will look there first.


WHAT THE OFFICE SEES
--------------------
A new "Charity collections" area in the Admin Centre, beside Hall Hire.
Administrators and office accounts, authenticator code required, same as
everywhere else.

Two things on that screen are deliberately NOT drawn like administration:

  1. A COLLECTOR WHO IS PAID gets a red badge and a tab of its own. On
     the paper form this is a tick in a box on page one that nobody reads
     twice. It is a safeguarding and fraud control — the committee should
     see it at a glance.

  2. TWO CHARITIES ON ONE DAY is flagged on the row. The masjid allows
     one collection a day. The public form is NOT told which days are
     taken, on purpose — that would be publishing the masjid's diary,
     the same decision already made for nikah.

A UK charity number is shown as a one-click link to the Charity
Commission register, so whoever reviews it can check in about five
seconds. An overseas registration number is shown plainly and marked
"check by hand" — a dead link to the Commission would read as "the
masjid checked and found nothing".


THE SIGNATURE
-------------
Typing a name has the same effect as signing the sheet. What makes it
evidence is that it is stored with the date and time AND with the version
of the rules that was on screen at that moment. If the masjid changes the
collection rules, every earlier request still says what that person
actually agreed to. The paper form cannot do that, because it does not
name its own version either.


THINGS ONLY THE MASJID CAN DECIDE
---------------------------------
1. FOURTEEN DAYS' NOTICE. That is what the form asks for now, matching
   what the masjid already asks for a nikah. Nobody has said whether a
   collection needs as long. If it should be less, tell me — it is one
   number, changed in two places, and both have to move together.

2. RAFIK PATEL'S NUMBER IS THE ONLY ONE ON THE FORM, as asked. Ali
   Khotiwala's is not on it anywhere.

3. THE DATA PROTECTION PAPERWORK. This form stores a TRUSTEE's name,
   phone and email — somebody who is not filling the form in and has not
   agreed to anything. That is the same point raised about the nikah
   witnesses. The DPIA, the ICO registration, the lawful-basis note and a
   privacy-notice line naming trustees are still outstanding for BOTH.
   The form now asks the applicant to confirm the trustee is content to
   be contacted, which helps and is not a substitute.
