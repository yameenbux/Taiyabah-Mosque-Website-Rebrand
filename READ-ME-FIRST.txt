BEFORE YOU TELL ANYONE THE PAGE IS OPEN — DO THIS
-------------------------------------------------
1. OPEN ALL 16 BUTTONS AND CHECK THE AMOUNT.

   I could not do this. Stripe blocks automated access to
   donate.stripe.com, so nothing here can read what is actually behind
   each URL. What was checked is that all 16 are different, none is a
   test-mode link, and all point at Stripe.

   What was NOT checked is whether the £100-every-Friday button really
   opens a £100 weekly page. If two links got swapped when they were
   copied, the page cannot tell and neither can I. Ten minutes with the
   live site open, pressing each of the 16 and reading the amount and
   the "per week / per month" line on Stripe's page, is the only way to
   know. Do it before the page is announced, not after.

2. MAKE ONE REAL PAYMENT.

   £1, real card, live mode. Watch it appear in Stripe, then watch the
   payout reach the bank. Refund it afterwards.

   No real money has ever gone through this Stripe account. Until one
   payment has, the whole thing is unproven — not the buttons, but
   whether the account can receive money at all and whether the payout
   details are right.

3. CHECK GIFT AID IS ON THE LINKS.

   The page tells donors: tick Yes on the payment page and the masjid
   reclaims 25p in the pound. If that question is not on these Payment
   Links, that sentence is untrue and should come off the page. Open one
   link and look. Tell me either way.
