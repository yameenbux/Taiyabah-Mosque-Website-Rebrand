THE DOCUMENT
------------
Fifteen pages, following the ICO's own seven-step DPIA structure so a regulator
recognises the shape of it. It is a DRAFT and deliberately unsigned.

It is not legal advice, and the person who wrote it is the person who built the
system — which makes it accurate about the software and no substitute for
somebody qualified reading it. Its job is to state exactly what happens to the
data so the trustees can make the judgements a DPIA requires.

Everything the masjid must decide is marked DECISION REQUIRED. Everything that
must be done before the form opens is marked ACTION, and all fourteen are
collected in one table in Step 7 with a blank for an owner and a date.

The four that will take real thought:

  * the Article 6 lawful basis and the Article 9 condition for health data;
  * whether a Data Protection Officer is legally required — arguable either way
    for a madrasah of 800 children, and the reasoning has to be recorded
    whichever way you land;
  * who is named as accountable, so a parent has somebody to ask;
  * whether to consult a few parents, or record why not.


ETHNICITY IS GONE
-----------------
You decided to remove it rather than justify it. That is done, in three places:

  * the field, its dropdown and the ONS group list are out of the form;
  * the payload no longer carries it;
  * migration 012 drops the column and rewrites the submission function.

012 refuses to run if any row holds an ethnicity value. None do — nothing has
ever been submitted — but on any other database that check is the difference
between a tidy-up and destroying data.

Run 012 in the SQL editor, then RUN 011 AGAIN. Same standing rule as before:
011 only protects policies that exist when it runs, and 012 rewrites a
function. I tested the whole sequence — 008, 009, 010, 011, 012, then 011 again
— and confirmed 17 policies protected and a real submission still succeeding
with the column gone.

This takes a whole row off the DPIA's risk register rather than mitigating it,
which is always the better trade when it is available.


SOMETHING I BROKE AND FIXED
---------------------------
While removing ethnicity from the browser test I wrote a careless one-liner
that emptied the test file instead of editing it. I rebuilt it from scratch,
larger than before: it now also asserts the preview cannot send, that ethnicity
stays gone from the form and from the payload, and that the honeypot still
works. All eight suites pass.

Telling you because a test file silently becoming empty is exactly the kind of
thing that looks like everything passing.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO
---------------------
It does not switch the form on, and nothing here brings that any closer on its
own. The form still cannot send, and 008 stays as it is. The DPIA has to be
completed and adopted first — that is the whole point of it.
