WHAT CHANGES
------------
   Paying the £100 deposit now books the date outright. The office no longer
   confirms a booking that has already been paid for — there is no Confirm
   button and no Decline button on a paid booking, because the masjid has
   somebody's money and the terms promise them the date.

   The portal now shows the money: the hall rate is worked out automatically
   from the date and the number of halls, the office types in the extras
   (utensils, catering), and the line reads

       £500 base + £154 extras - £100 deposit = £554 outstanding

   A booking taken under the old session rates has no rate on file and says so
   rather than showing £0.

   There is one way to undo a paid booking: "Cancel & refund". It asks why,
   records the reason in the audit log, marks the £100 as owed back, and frees
   the date. It is deliberately slower than a button click, because it always
   means sending money back to somebody.
