box. It asks for their reference, then gives two buttons — Member £100 and
Non-member £200.

    "Once the office has rung you and agreed your date. Paying does not book a

WHAT THE OFFICE SEES
--------------------
Nothing changes about how a nikāḥ is agreed. A request that has paid its fee
still shows "Agree date" and "Decline" in the portal, because the payment
agrees nothing — the masjid does not publish its nikāḥ diary and the website
cannot know whether a date is free.

The row shows "fee paid", the amount, and which rate it was — so a family who
picked the wrong one is visible rather than silently short.
