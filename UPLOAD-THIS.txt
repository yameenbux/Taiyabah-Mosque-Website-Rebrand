WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
The notice at the top of "Check availability & book" still said:

  "This sends a request, not a booking. The date is not held until the office
   has rung you back, agreed it and taken the deposit."

Every clause of that is now false. Nobody rings you back, nobody in the office
agrees the date, and the deposit is not taken over the phone. Worse, it told a
hirer their date was not held when in fact it is — for thirty minutes while
they pay.


WHAT IT SAYS NOW
----------------
  "Paying the £100 deposit books the date. Choose your day, fill in the short
   form and pay — the date is yours as soon as the deposit goes through, with
   nobody to wait for. It is held for you for thirty minutes while you pay.
   Prefer to arrange it by phone? Call 01204 535 997."

Two buttons were renamed to match what they actually do:

  the calendar button   "Request"            ->  "Book"
  the form button       "Send this request"  ->  "Continue to the £100 deposit"

The privacy notice's reason for asking for a phone number was also corrected:
the office rings to confirm the balance and arrange access, not to agree the
booking.
