TAIYABAH MASJID — paying the nikāḥ fee online, 7 September 2026
==============================================================

FOUR STEPS. Steps 1 and 2 can be done in any order; 3 and 4 come last.


1. MAKE TWO STRIPE PAYMENT LINKS
--------------------------------
   In Stripe, create two Payment Links:

       Nikah fee - member       £100
       Nikah fee - non-member   £200

   Set them up as payments for SERVICES, the same way you did the £100 hall
   deposit. NOT as donations. A nikāḥ fee is not a gift, and running it
   through a donation link misstates it in the charity's accounts and risks a
   Gift Aid problem.

   Copy both URLs. You need them in step 3.


2. RUN THE SQL
--------------
   Supabase -> SQL Editor -> paste the whole of db/018_nikah_fee_online.sql
   -> Run.

   Then paste the whole of db/011_require_two_step.sql and run that too.
   (Standing rule: 011 goes again after every migration.)


3. PUT THE TWO LINKS INTO THE PAGE
----------------------------------
   Open index.html and find this, near the bottom:

       var NIKAH_LINKS = {
         member:     '',   // £100
         non_member: ''    // £200
       };

   Paste the two URLs between the quote marks. Until you do, the "Pay the fee
   online" box does not appear at all and the page just shows cash and bank
   transfer as before — so uploading before you have the links is safe.

   Send me the two URLs if you would rather I put them in and rebuild
   properly; index.html is generated from a template and hand-editing it is
   normally the wrong thing to do.


4. UPLOAD THE FILES
-------------------
   Repository root, replacing what is there:

       index.html
       404.html
       README.md

   Inside the venue folder:

       venue/app.js
       venue/index.html

   Then redeploy the webhook. It now handles both kinds of payment:

       supabase functions deploy stripe-webhook --no-verify-jwt

   (The file is in supabase-function/ in this zip — it belongs at
   supabase/functions/stripe-webhook/index.ts in your project.)

   Do NOT upload the db/ folder.


WHAT THIS DOES — AND DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO
---------------------------------------------
   A family can now pay the nikāḥ fee online instead of bringing cash to the
   office. They pick £100 (member) or £200 (non-member) and type the
   reference from their request; the office checks the rate on the call.

   PAYING DOES NOT BOOK A DATE. This is the opposite of the hall, and it is
   on purpose. The hall calendar knows what is free, so paying can sell a
   date outright. The masjid does not publish its nikāḥ diary, so the website
   has no idea whether any given day is available — taking £100 for a date
   nobody has checked would be selling something the masjid cannot promise.

   So the sequence is unchanged: family requests a date, office rings, office
   agrees it. The only new thing is how the fee gets paid.

   In the portal, a nikāḥ that has paid its fee still shows "Agree date" and
   "Decline". That is correct. If those ever disappear, something has gone
   wrong.


NEW THINGS IN THE PORTAL
------------------------
   - a "fee paid" badge on nikāḥ rows, and which rate was paid, so a family
     who picked the wrong one is visible
   - "£100 received" / "£200 received" buttons, for a fee taken in cash or by
     bank transfer
   - the Refunds tab now shows nikāḥ fees owed back as well as hall deposits.
     Before this, a nikāḥ refund was invisible.


IF SOMEONE PAYS TWICE
---------------------
   Two family members both paying is ordinary. The first payment is kept
   exactly as it is, the second is written to the audit log naming both
   Stripe payments, and nothing is overwritten. Somebody refunds one. Find
   them with:

       select * from public.admin_audit
        where action = 'nikah_fee_paid_twice' order by at desc;
