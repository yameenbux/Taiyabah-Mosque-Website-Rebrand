ONE THING WORTH A CONVERSATION WITH THE COMMITTEE — NOT A CODE PROBLEM
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Giving every member of staff `admin` works, and for a handful of trustees it is
a reasonable call. This is not me asking you to undo it. It is me making sure
the trade is on the record, because it is the kind of thing that is obvious in
hindsight and invisible beforehand.

`admin` reads EVERYTHING. Today that is hall bookings and nikāḥ requests, which
is unremarkable. But the madrasah system will hold roughly 800 children's
records — names, dates of birth, and in time medical and special-educational-
needs notes. That last category is special-category data under UK GDPR, and it
carries a higher bar.

Migration 004 was written for exactly this reason, and its own comment says it
plainly: "the person who answers the phone about hiring a hall for a wedding has
no business being able to open a child's file, and giving them that access would
make the masjid's data protection position much harder to defend."

The question is not technical. It is: is every person holding `admin` somebody
the masjid would be content to name, in writing, to the ICO as having lawful
access to every child's record?

If the answer is yes — a few trustees, all DBS-checked, all with a reason to be
there — then this is fine, and the useful thing is simply to write down that it
was a decision rather than an accident. Regulators care far more about a
documented judgement than a perfect one.

If the answer is ever "not quite" — a volunteer who only does hall bookings, a
teacher who only needs their own class — the narrower roles are all still there.
Nothing was deleted. Every policy still honours hall_office and teacher, the
tests still prove hall_office sees zero madrasah data, and granting one instead
is a single line in STAFF_give_someone_a_role.sql.

Two practical knock-ons while you are deciding:

  * Migration 011 requires EVERY account holding admin to have an authenticator
    set up before it will apply. More admins means more people to walk through
    that. It is a hurdle worth clearing, not a reason to delay.

  * Keep at least two administrators at all times. Deleting the only one
    destroys its roles and profile by cascade, and nobody is left who can grant
    the role back.

I have added this to the README under the security rules, so it is somewhere
the next person will find it rather than only in this note.
