BUT I DID NOT JUST FLIP TWO SWITCHES
------------------------------------
Before turning them on I read the privacy notice. It said this:

    "The only thing this website collects is a hall hire request."

The moment those two forms went live that sentence became false, on the one page
whose entire job is to be true. Both forms carry a privacy tick pointing at a
notice that did not describe what they collect — an Article 13 failure as well
as a wrong statement.

So the notice is rewritten. It now describes all four things the site collects,
each under its own heading:

  * a hall hire request
  * a nikāḥ date request — including that we deliberately do NOT ask for the
    couple's names, addresses, documents or witnesses
  * a place on an adult class, and the waiting list
  * a shop account

with the lawful basis for each, who can see it, that staff need an authenticator
code and that the database now enforces that rather than the website, and how
long each is kept.


AND SOMETHING I FOUND WHILE IN THERE
------------------------------------
The hall hire form — the oldest on the site, the one taking real requests from
real people — had NO privacy statement at all. No line about what happens to the
data, no link to the notice, nothing. Every other form had one.

It now carries a short paragraph: what the data is used for, that it is not
shared, that requests are deleted after six months, and a link to the notice.

Deliberately not a tick box. The basis for a hall booking is taking steps before
entering a contract, not consent, so a box people tick unread adds friction and
no protection.

That gap predates this week's work. It is closed now.
