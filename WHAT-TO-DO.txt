What changed
------------
1. Two weeks' notice. The calendar starts a fortnight from today; the days in
   between are shown struck through so it is obvious why, and the database
   refuses them again on the way in — a browser can be edited, the database
   cannot.

2. Times are prayers, not hours. The picker offers After Fajr, Zuhr, Asr,
   Maghrib and Isha, and reads the real jamāʿah time for that date out of the
   masjid's own 2026 timetable rather than inventing one. On a Saturday it also
   offers 11:00am, for a nikāḥ held before a separate wedding meal. There is an
   "I'm flexible" option for couples who would rather the masjid chose.

   Choosing Saturday 11am and then moving to a weekday clears the time, so
   nobody can submit an 11am on a Tuesday. The database enforces the same rule.

3. One list for the office. The Astley Hall admin page now shows hall bookings
   and nikāḥ requests together, each with a badge saying which it is. Nikāḥ rows
   say "Agree date" rather than "Confirm", because the office still has to ring
   and take payment. Writes go to the right table with the right timestamp
   column — nothing is mixed up between the two.

   If 010 has not been applied yet, the page notices the missing table and
   carries on showing hall bookings, with no error thrown at the office.
