Gift Aid to claim

Every donation where the donor said yes and the masjid has not claimed yet. Copy it into HMRC’s spreadsheet, submit the claim, then mark them claimed here so nothing is ever claimed twice.
How to file the claim
  1. Download HMRC’s own Gift Aid schedule spreadsheet from gov.uk — search for “schedule spreadsheet to claim back tax on Gift Aid donations”. Use theirs, not a copy: the sheet has to be named exactly right or Charities Online refuses the attachment.
  2. Press Copy for HMRC above, then click the first empty donor row in their spreadsheet and paste. The columns line up.
  3. Save it as .ods and attach it to your claim in Charities Online. Do not convert it between Excel and LibreOffice formats — HMRC warn that this breaks the attachment.
  4. Come back here and press Mark all as claimed, so the same donations are never claimed twice.

HMRC take a maximum of 1,000 donations per spreadsheet. If there are more than that here, claim in batches — copy, file, mark claimed, repeat.
