Hall hire charges

This is the rate card on the hall hire page of the website — the line at the top, the groups of charges with their prices, and the booking team’s names and numbers. There is no draft here. Notices and the prayer timetable wait for you to publish them; this one does not. Whatever you save is on the website the moment you save it, so read the preview at the bottom first.

The £100 deposit and the bank details are not on this screen

They sit on the same page, just under the charges, and they stay exactly where they are. The database refuses to save a rate card that so much as mentions either of them, so this is not simply a box that was left off the form.

The deposit is a fixed Stripe payment link. The £100 lives at Stripe, not on the website. Change the number here and the button underneath would still take £100 — the page would be telling somebody a price the masjid does not charge, in the one place where being wrong costs an argument with a customer rather than an apology.

Changing it for real means creating a new payment link, which needs Yameen and the Stripe account. Ring the office and ask; it is not something that can be done from here.

Groups of charges

Between one and six groups, and between one and ten charges in each. Every price has to have a number in it — “£350” and “45p per person” are both fine, “ask in the office” is not. If a charge depends on the booking, take the line out rather than leaving it vague.

Six groups is as many as the rate card will hold. Remove one before adding another.

Who to ring about a booking

Up to eight people. The office number is printed on the page by the website itself, so this list is the people somebody should actually ring.

Eight contacts is the most the page will take. Remove one before adding another.
