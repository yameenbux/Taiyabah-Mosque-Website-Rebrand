In-mosque displays

Pick a screen below to see what's on it now and change it. The appeal, the donate link and the progress bar are the foyer TVs' own; the Ramadan and Eid greeting is shared — switch it on from any screen and it shows on every TV in the mosque. There is no draft: submitting a change commits it to the screens themselves, and each TV picks it up within about twenty minutes — it does not need switching off and on.
