Add or replace a year

Upload the masjid’s spreadsheet and the times are read straight out of it. If you would rather paste them in, it is one row a day, in this order:

date, hijri, fajr begins, fajr jamāʿah, sunrise, zuhr begins, zuhr jamāʿah, asr begins, asr jamāʿah, maghrib, isha begins, isha jamāʿah, jumuʿah

The date can be 2027-01-01 or 01/01/2027. Times must be 24-hour, like 06:35. Leave the Jumuʿah column empty except on Fridays, where it is the two times separated by a comma: 13:15,14:00. A heading row is ignored.
