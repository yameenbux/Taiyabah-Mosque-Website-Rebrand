Sending a message to parents

Writing to parents about a closure, a trip or a change of time is not built yet. It needs a change to the part of the system that sends every email the masjid sends — bookings, invitations, receipts, fee reminders — and that is worth doing carefully rather than quickly.

Until then, this screen prints letters, and the office has the families’ telephone numbers on the Families screen.
